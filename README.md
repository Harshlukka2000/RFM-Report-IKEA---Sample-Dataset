# IKEA Customer Segmentation & Churn Analysis (SQL, Python, Power BI)

Customer retention, RFM segmentation, and revenue analysis across 5,881 retail customers using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

*Note: Built on a sample retail dataset adapted for an IKEA-themed case study (not official IKEA data).*

## Project Workflow
* **SQL (sql/):** Cleaned raw transaction data and aggregated customer purchase metrics[cite: 9].
* **Python (python/):** Calculated Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) scores, segmented customers, and flagged churn (IsChurned).
* **Power BI (powerbi/):** Built a themed interactive dashboard (`IKEA Project Theme.json`) with KPI cards, slicers, churn trend lines, and bookmark navigators[cite: 1, 3, 4, 9].

## Key Findings
* **5,881 total customers** generating **~$17.37M** in revenue[cite: 5].
* **Champions (1,291 customers)** drive **$11.84M** (~68% of total revenue)[cite: 5].
* **Loyal (1,416 customers)** bring in **$2.70M**[cite: 5].
* **At Risk (821 customers)** and **Needs Attention (387 customers)** hold **$1.79M** combined in potential repeat revenue before slipping into **Lost (1,524 customers)**[cite: 5].
